RapideVite verified packshots — starter batch

This starter batch contains 8 exact, real product packshots identified from your market photos.

How to use on your Mac:
1. Unzip this file.
2. Open Terminal.
3. Type: cd 
4. Drag the unzipped folder into Terminal and press Return.
5. Run:
   python3 download_packshots.py
6. A folder named product_images will be created.
7. Zip product_images together with products_manifest.csv and upload that ZIP to Claude.

These are real retailer packshots, not AI-generated images and not shelf crops.

Important:
- Use them as draft catalog images.
- Confirm the exact package size sold in your market before publishing.
- Keep source_page and image_url in your records for attribution and replacement.
